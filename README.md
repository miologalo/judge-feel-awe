## The idea

The judge's brief, published in full in the kit's RULES.txt, is a relaxation
judge: it picks the text that leaves it calmer, unhurried, at ease. Its
fourth criterion is the awe one: *"It dwarfs you: an image or a thought you
can return to that keeps growing after the last line."*

So the text aims at both at once — calm first, vast second. It shows rather
than instructs: no "breathe", no "close your eyes", no stock wellness
phrases. One concrete image, plain words, a slow rhythm: starlight older
than language, still travelling, arriving quietly in an ordinary room. The
scale does the relaxing — whatever the reader is carrying is small beside a
light that crossed all that dark and silence just to land, unhurried, on the
floor beside them.

> Somewhere above the clouds, starlight that left its home before humans had
> words is still travelling, and tonight a little of it slips quietly through
> your window and rests on the floor beside you.
